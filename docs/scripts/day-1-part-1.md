# Day 1 · Part 1: Short answers about you

Sarah explains the test, then teaches one habit for Part 1: answer, then add one more sentence. The learner answers one question, then hears Aarav answer the same one.

**Prototype:** https://ielts-day1.vercel.app  
**Sections:** 20  
**Voice-over:** 59 lines, about 3m 07s of speech, plus the time the learner spends answering  
**Audio files:** 59

## Voices

| Voice | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Sarah** | the teacher. Warm, clear, unhurried. Talking to one person. |
| **Aarav** | another student. Young man, Indian English, relaxed and natural. Heard once, in section 17. |

## How to read this

- Each **section** is one screen in the prototype, in the order the learner sees it.
- Each **row** is one line of speech and becomes **one audio file**. Keep every row as its own clip. Its length decides when the caption changes and when that line's animation happens. Two rows merged into one file would put the animations out of step.
- File names: `d1-<section>-<line>.mp3`.
- Sections marked *no voice-over* are where the learner does something: tapping, writing or speaking.
